Privacy & cookies

What we collect when you join the Loqelo waiting list, why we collect it, and the control you have over it.

Last updated: 24 October 2026 · This policy is currently available in English. Where it is translated, the English version prevails.

1. Who we are

Loqelo is a service marketplace connecting customers with service providers for the home, garden and office. The platform is in pre-launch; this website exists to tell you about the project and to collect a waiting list.

2. What we collect

We keep this deliberately short. If you join the waiting list, we collect:

  • Your email address — so we can write to you about the launch.
  • Your country — so we know where interest is coming from and can tell you when we reach your market.
  • Whether you are an individual or a business, and whether you are a customer or a provider — so we send you the relevant information.
  • A record of your consent — the moment you agreed, the wording you saw and the page you were on. We need this to prove consent if asked.
  • Basic technical data — the page you signed up from, our referrer (how you found us), any campaign parameters in the link, and your language setting.

If you only browse the site and decline analytics, we do not store anything about you at all.

3. Why we process it, and on what legal basis

  • Sending launch news and updates — legal basis: your consent (GDPR art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Understanding which countries show interest — legal basis: our legitimate interest in planning the launch (art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Keeping the forms secure and free of spam — legal basis: legitimate interest (art. 6(1)(f)) and, where required, our legal obligations (art. 6(1)(c)).

We never use your email address or the content of your form submission for advertising, and we do not use it to build profiles.

4. Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site performs: how many people visit, which pages they read, and how many people complete the sign-up form. It is switched on only after you press “Accept” in the cookie banner. If you decline, no analytics script is loaded and no analytics cookie is written.

We have configured analytics so that:

  • IP addresses are anonymised before storage;
  • your email address and the values you type into our forms are never sent to Google;
  • country statistics come from Google's approximate, IP-derived location, not from the country you select in our form;
  • advertising and personalisation signals are permanently disabled;
  • event data is retained for 14 months.

Google Ireland Limited is the provider. Where data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, the transfer relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. See Google's privacy policy.

6. Who else processes your data

  • Hosting and forms — our website host stores the form submissions and provides the server infrastructure. Submissions are held in the provider's form inbox, exportable to CSV only by us.
  • Email — if we send you launch updates, they are sent through an email service provider acting on our instructions.
  • Analytics — Google Ireland Limited, as described in section 4, and only after consent.

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it with advertisers. We may disclose data where we are legally required to do so.

7. How long we keep it

  • Waiting-list contact data: until the launch is complete and for no longer than 24 months after you sign up, unless you ask us to delete it sooner or you unsubscribe.
  • Unsubscribe and consent records: we keep a minimal record of when consent was given or withdrawn, so that we can demonstrate compliance.
  • Analytics data: 14 months.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you, and receive a copy;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data deleted (the “right to be forgotten”);
  • restrict or object to our processing;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of what happened before;
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Poland, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO); in the Netherlands, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

To exercise any of these, write to privacy@loqelo.com. We answer within 30 days.

9. Unsubscribing

Every email we send contains a one-click unsubscribe link. You can also reply to any message with “unsubscribe”, or email us directly. Unsubscribing removes you from the waiting list and from all launch communication.

10. Security

The site is served over HTTPS only. Form submissions are protected against automated abuse by a hidden bot trap, submission-timing checks and rate limiting, and the form provider applies its own spam filtering. Access to the collected data is limited to the people who need it to run the launch.

11. Children

The waiting list is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has signed up, contact us and we will delete the record.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal data, we update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes affecting people already on the waiting list will be announced by email.
