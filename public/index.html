How Loqelo works

From one sentence to the right pro.

No endless searching. No five phone calls for a quote. Four steps, in one place, at your pace.

  1. 01

    Describe your need

    Write it the way you'd say it: “my hedge is overgrown and the windows need doing”. Add photos if it helps.

  2. 02

    We find the pros

    Loqelo works out which services you need and puts the job in front of verified providers nearby.

  3. 03

    Compare the offers

    Price, timing, ratings and completion rates side by side — plus a Q&A thread for the details.

  4. 04

    Choose and book

    Pick the offer that fits, agree the details and get it done. Your address is only shared after you choose.

“I need the gutters cleared and a new fence panel before winter.” That's the whole job post.
